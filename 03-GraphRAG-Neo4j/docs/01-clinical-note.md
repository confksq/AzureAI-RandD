# Clinical Note — Northside Rheumatology

**Patient:** Maria Delgado (MRN 88-4102)
**Date of visit:** 2026-03-14
**Attending:** Dr. Priya Raman, Northside Rheumatology

## Assessment

Maria Delgado is a 47-year-old with seropositive rheumatoid arthritis, diagnosed
2023-08. Disease activity remains moderate-to-high (DAS28 5.1) despite twelve
months of methotrexate at 25 mg weekly. She reports persistent morning stiffness
exceeding ninety minutes and bilateral MCP joint swelling.

Methotrexate is documented as an inadequate response — not an intolerance. She
tolerated the drug without significant hepatotoxicity, but failed to reach
low disease activity. Hydroxychloroquine was added 2025-01 as combination therapy
and also failed to produce remission.

## Plan

Escalate to a biologic DMARD. Dr. Raman is initiating **adalimumab** 40 mg
subcutaneous every other week. Patient is covered under the MeridianCare
Commercial plan, so this prescription requires prior authorization before the
specialty pharmacy will dispense.

Baseline TB screening (QuantiFERON) is negative and hepatitis B surface antigen
is negative, both drawn 2026-03-10.
