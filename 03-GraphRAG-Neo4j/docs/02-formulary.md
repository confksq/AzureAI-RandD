# MeridianCare Commercial Formulary — 2026 Edition

## Tier Structure

| Tier | Description | Member cost share | Prior auth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Preferred generics | $10 copay | No |
| Tier 2 | Preferred brand | $45 copay | No |
| Tier 3 | Specialty / biologic | 30% coinsurance | Yes — always |
| Tier 4 | Non-formulary | Not covered | Exception only |

## Tier 3 Specialty Listings — Immunology

**Adalimumab** (Humira and biosimilars) — Tier 3 Specialty. Prior authorization
required under policy **PA-114**. Step therapy applies: the member must have a
documented inadequate response to at least one conventional synthetic DMARD.
Methotrexate is the designated first-line agent for rheumatoid arthritis.

**Etanercept** — Tier 3 Specialty. Also governed by PA-114. Adalimumab is the
preferred agent within the TNF-inhibitor class; etanercept requires an
additional preferred-agent exception.

**Methotrexate** — Tier 1. No prior authorization. Designated step-therapy
prerequisite for all Tier 3 immunology biologics.

**Hydroxychloroquine** — Tier 1. No prior authorization. May be used as
combination therapy but does not by itself satisfy the PA-114 step requirement.

## Specialty Pharmacy

All Tier 3 immunology drugs dispense exclusively through MeridianCare Specialty
Pharmacy Network. Retail pharmacy claims for these agents reject at point of
sale with code SP-01.

## Formulary Governance

This formulary is maintained by the MeridianCare Pharmacy & Therapeutics
Committee and revised annually. Mid-year additions require P&T Committee
approval and are published as formulary bulletins.
