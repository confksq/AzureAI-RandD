# MeridianCare Utilization Management — Policy PA-114

**Title:** Prior Authorization — Tier 3 Specialty Biologics (Immunology)
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** MeridianCare Clinical Review Board

## Scope

PA-114 governs prior authorization for all Tier 3 Specialty biologic agents in
the immunology class, including adalimumab and etanercept, as listed in the
MeridianCare Commercial Formulary.

## Approval Criteria

A request is approved when all four criteria are met:

1. **Diagnosis** — Confirmed rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, or
   ankylosing spondylitis, documented with ICD-10 code and a disease activity
   score.
2. **Step therapy** — Documented inadequate response or intolerance to at least
   one conventional synthetic DMARD, with a minimum three-month trial.
   Methotrexate satisfies this requirement.
3. **Prescriber qualification** — The prescribing physician must be a
   board-certified rheumatologist, or the request must carry a rheumatology
   consultation note. Requests from non-specialists are denied under code
   PRESCRIBER-02.
4. **Safety screening** — Negative tuberculosis screening and hepatitis B
   surface antigen within ninety days of the request.

## Turnaround

Standard determinations are issued within five business days. Expedited
requests, where delay would jeopardize the member's health, are determined
within seventy-two hours.

## Denial and Appeal Path

Denials are issued by the MeridianCare Clinical Review Board with a specific
denial code. Members and prescribers may file a first-level appeal within
sixty days.

First-level appeals are reviewed by the **MeridianCare Clinical Review Board**.
Appeals that uphold the original denial escalate to the plan medical director,
**Dr. Alan Whitfield**, for second-level review. Second-level determinations are
issued within thirty days.

Appeals must include the original denial letter, updated clinical documentation,
and evidence addressing the specific denial code cited. For step-therapy denials
(code STEP-01) the appeal must document the prior DMARD trial, its duration, and
the disease activity score demonstrating inadequate response.

Members exhausting both internal levels may request external independent review
through the state insurance commissioner.
